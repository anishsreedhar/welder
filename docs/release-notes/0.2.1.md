Welder 0.2.1: the GPU finally switched on in the Windows and Linux downloads, four Psychic Rendering
nodes, and two more batches of film looks.

## Fixed

- **The GPU was never used in the downloads.** The Windows and Linux builds were made without
  Welder's GPU code, so **Use the GPU** in Preferences couldn't be ticked and Denoise, Transform,
  Defocus, Blur and the rest always ran on the CPU. They now include it. A build without GPU code
  says "not in this build", and the Performance panel shows the real GPU status. The setting is
  saved under a new name, so the GPU comes on after updating. (#46, #60)

## New: Psychic Rendering

Four motion-aware Stylize and Time nodes. Feed them VectorGenerator's vectors to make the effect
follow the picture. `examples/psychic.weld` chains all four. (#59, #61, #62)

- **FlowBreath** pushes the plate around with curl noise, which swirls without bunching up or
  tearing. Detail lock keeps flat areas still, and flow adhesion makes the noise ride on moving
  surfaces.
- **FractalFold** applies recursive kaleidoscope folds around a centre or on a grid of tiles, with
  mirror, grow, shift and turn steps between iterations.
- **SpectralPhase** splits red and blue along a noise field and swings hue and chroma in OKLCH,
  keeping lightness exactly.
- **TemporalTracer** leaves trails from 1 to 24 earlier frames, only where things move, with
  decay, tint, a hue shift per frame and add, screen or max blending.

## New: film looks

- **Lens imperfections** in Defocus and ZDefocus: cat-eye (with swirl), onion rings, rim and colour
  fringe. **Halation** gets a scene-linear mode and a two-colour halo. **LensFlare** has 12 new
  looks, **Noise** gains bubbles, puffy and bumpy cells, and there are new **WhipTransition** and
  **PrismLens** nodes. (#63)
- **PixelMosh**, a datamosh node: from a cut on, the last clean picture is dragged block by block
  along the new shot's motion. Each **VHS** module can now be switched off, and there are new tape
  crease, dropout, colour-under and edge-ringing modules (off by default). (#64)
